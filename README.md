# Squeek Rescue Station

A pixel-art puzzle game where you guide hamsters home through a maze of conveyor belts.

Rotate arrows, route your crew around obstacles, and rescue as many pals as you can during a two-minute shift. Every new game brings randomized arrows and obstacles, with a route from the entrance to home.

Live wheel activity from Squeekchain powers your battery. When the wheel rests, use the hand crank—but watch the heat. Personal high scores stay in your browser.
